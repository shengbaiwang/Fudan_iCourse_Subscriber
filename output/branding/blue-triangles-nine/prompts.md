# iCourse · Final prompts for nine drafts

## 01 · 余隙

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: one independent square app-icon design draft for iCourse, a private lifelong learning archive.
Two supplied images are aesthetic references only: reference 1 contributes simple crisp postmodern geometry and controlled misalignment; reference 2 contributes deep monochrome blue, material density and contemplative breathing space. Invent a NEW silhouette matching the variant brief; do not copy the four-piece arrangement of either reference.
Literary concept: Anne Carson's Short Talk on the Total Collection, particularly blue triangles, the urge to preserve and classify everything, the missing piece, and classification becoming waves. Convey this tension poetically through geometry and negative space. Do not illustrate Noah, an ark, flood, books or shelves.
Visual language: original postmodern trademark with the quiet discipline of Korean monochrome painting, reduction, repetition, meaningful absence, monochrome pigment. Use large simple TRUE TRIANGLES only. Crisp straight edges, strong silhouette, carefully proportioned gaps. Elegance before decoration.
Composition: one emblem optically centered on a full-bleed square canvas, occupying approximately 54–60% of the canvas, with ample safety margins on all sides. Designed to be readable at 32px. No enclosing rounded-square tile.
Material: nearly flat matte pigment with extremely subtle density variation, texture visible only on close inspection. Do not use the heavy grain of reference 2. No glossy depth.
Constraints: no lettering, no numbers, no wordmark, no caption, no extra symbols, no border, no grid/board/mockup, no phone. No glow, gradients, shadows, bevels, glass, photorealism, rainbow accents, brushstroke clutter or worn edges. No generic tech networks, play button, recycling symbol or literal mountain scenery. Deliver ONE usable square icon, not a presentation.
VARIANT 01 — 余隙:
Create FOUR broad, unequal scalene triangles arranged into a compact, roughly square emblem around a small off-center irregular diamond-shaped negative-space cavity. Each triangle remains visibly separate with clean broad gaps. The cavity is the emotional center. Slight asymmetry, poised and calm, no rotational recycling pattern. A quiet collection that contains an intentional absence.
Palette: Dense muted Prussian/indigo blue triangles #1C3D72 on warm pale ivory #F1EEE6. Only two close blue pigment densities, never multicolored.
```
