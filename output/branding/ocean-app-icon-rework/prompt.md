# iCourse 海洋应用图标

生成方式：内置 imagegen。

完整提示词：

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production-oriented iOS app icon for iCourse, a private archive for reading and learning.
Primary request: create one beautiful, sophisticated APP ICON combining blue triangles with the feeling of the ocean. Design for the home screen at 48–64 px, with a bold memorable silhouette, optical balance, and exceptional simplicity.
Composition: one square full-bleed icon asset, straight-on, no presentation board. Background is an unbroken deep inky ultramarine blue color field. In the middle, ONE large compact emblem occupying about 65% of the canvas. Generous even padding.
Emblem: invent a unified folded triangular ocean crest. Two broad triangular planes JOIN into one cohesive asymmetric silhouette with one apex rising slightly right of center. One clean, sweeping concave negative-space cut travels upward through the lower half, suggesting a quiet swell of ocean water. The lower plane folds back gently. A triangle remains the underlying organizing geometry, but the result feels like a collected fragment of sea and wind. Keep the shape broad, substantial and instantly readable; only one internal opening and at most two joined planes. No scattered triangles.
Visual language: elegant contemporary postmodern identity, restraint and contemplative depth from Korean monochrome abstraction. Crisp, meticulously controlled edges, subtly softened points, strong foreground-background value contrast. Mostly flat color; a very restrained tonal transition may give the blue fold a little depth. Surface is smooth matte, absolutely no paper texture, no visible noise. Artistic and poetic yet unmistakably a finished app icon.
Palette: midnight ocean blue background, mineral blue main plane, a slightly lighter porcelain-blue upper plane. Blue-on-blue, with sufficient lightness contrast for a tiny app icon. The palette should feel luminous within deep water, never neon. No white.
Constraints: the output is the icon artwork itself filling a square canvas. No text, no letter i, no wordmark, no border, no drop shadow outside the emblem, no device mockup, no extra objects, no books, no horizon landscape. Do not draw nine choices or a design sheet. Do not copy a known app brand.
Avoid: abstract art collage, scattered polygons, sailboat pictogram, origami paper boat, propeller, recycling arrows, generic three-point triangular logo, glassmorphism, metallic 3D rendering, ornate gradients, excessive detail, photographic ocean, grain.
